# Tagryn's principles

Tagryn helps people understand and deliberately change the metadata in their own files. It is maintained by Franz Gollhammer, with contributions from the community.

## Your files remain yours

Core workflows run locally without an account, telemetry, or automatic map requests. The files and their sidecars remain the source of truth. A cache helps the interface; it must not become a proprietary catalog required to recover your work.

## Make changes visible

Inspection, unsaved drafts, saved changes, and restoration are distinct actions. Show the intended difference before writing. Verify backups and reread the result. Surface partial failures and format limits instead of promising universal safety or complete anonymization.

## Choose a coherent product

Build a useful desktop workbench for inspecting, comparing, and editing metadata. Favor understandable defaults, keyboard access, and a clear interface. Features should serve this workflow; every possible ExifTool option does not need to become a UI control.

## Welcome people using AI

AI is welcome in implementation, tests, documentation, investigation, and review. Contributors still own their submissions: understand the change, verify it, respect source licenses, and respond to feedback. Small, well-explained contributions make review sustainable. Tool choice neither guarantees nor prevents acceptance.

## Keep direction accountable

Franz Gollhammer has final responsibility for product direction, releases, and merge decisions. Proposals belong in issues or discussions; changes arrive through reviewed pull requests. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) expresses priorities, not delivery promises.

Omarchy's [doctrine](https://omarchy.org/doctrine/) inspired the emphasis on opinionated software and welcoming agents. Tagryn is an independent project with its own scope and implementation.
